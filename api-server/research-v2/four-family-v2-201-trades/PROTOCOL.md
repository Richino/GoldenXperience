# Frozen 201-trade V2 entry retest

Research-only versions. No V1 parameter changes. Freeze this document before results. The source is the 201 family trades in the September 5 review ledger; exclude the 29 legacy trades. This is an event-anchored retest of already seen trades, not a full-universe backtest or unseen validation. All 201 opportunities remain in the denominator, including skipped and data-incomplete cases.

Compare recorded journal R, confirmed broker fill price R, a common M1 replay control using the recorded executed entry/direction/stop/target, and delayed-entry V2. Long entries use ask, exits bid; short entries bid, exits ask. Every new entry is at the first M1 open at/after the completed confirmation. Charge an additional 0.1 pip adverse slippage on new replay entries/exits in both control and V2; also report a 0.5-pip stress. Do not add synthetic spread to executable prices. Conservatively resolve same-minute competing levels as stop-first; gap stops fill at the adverse open. Do not fill gaps or label partial data as resolved.

Entry confirmation uses only completed M15 midpoint candles. At the frozen opportunity time t, the last M15 bar is [t-15m,t). Wait at most four subsequent M15 bars (one hour). Recheck the ordinary session window and pair spread ceiling at delayed entry. Close remaining positions at 16:45 New York time that same day. Do not extend the original session because of delayed entry. Keep the original absolute stop/target for same-direction EMA/breakout/mean-reversion entries; reject invalid geometry or a worse reward/risk than V1. No trailing, breakeven, partial exits, or target tuning.

- EMA V2: anchor the original signal candle as the pullback candidate. Require a later M15 close beyond that candle's directional high/low while the stop remains intact. Enter next executable minute.
- Breakout V2: freeze the stored pre-break range level. Require a later M15 candle to touch/cross back to that level, then a subsequent, distinct M15 candle to close back beyond it in the original direction. Invalidate if the original stop is reached before entry. This is genuinely post-break confirmation.
- Momentum V2 continuation arm: use the raw, pre-inversion signal direction. Freeze the high/low of the 20 completed M15 bars preceding the signal bar. Require the signal bar to have closed outside that level; otherwise WAIT. Require a later candle to touch the level and close beyond it in the raw direction. This retest holds the continuation level.
- Momentum V2 failed-break arm: same raw breakout prerequisite; require a later completed candle to close back inside the frozen range. Enter opposite the raw direction. Preserve original risk distance and reward multiple (2R) around the new side's executable entry. Report continuation and failed-break separately; use the earliest qualifying event in the combined policy and never pick the better realized outcome.
- Mean reversion V2: freeze the range of the 20 bars preceding the signal bar. Within the following four bars require price to have pierced the extreme opposite the reversion direction (including the signal bar), then close back inside that frozen range in the reversion direction. Retain the original non-trending qualification and fixed mean target; reclassify regime from completed candles at delayed entry. Reject a new trending regime. Require at least the V1 minimum 1R available to the mean.

Report all-opportunity R (WAIT contributes 0; missing data stays missing), executed-entry R, matched control comparison, family counts, coverage, cost stress, chronology, and overlap. For an executable portfolio comparison, only one candidate position per instrument may be open; processing must be chronological and unresolved positions block subsequent entries. Report independent family arms as well as the portfolio. Do not call the final chronological subset OOS: these outcomes were already inspected. No thresholds are revised after this run.
