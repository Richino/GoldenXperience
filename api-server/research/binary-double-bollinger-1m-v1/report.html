Anatomy of a Coin Flip

A two-year forensic test of a 1-minute binary FX strategy — five indicators, twelve pairs, 208,000 trades — and the plain reason it never beats chance.

Method BB(20,2/3) · Heikin-Ashi · Stoch(6,3,3) · CCI(21) · ADX Window Aug 2024 → Aug 2026 Settlement real OANDA M1 mids
NO EDGE

1-minute binary direction is a coin flip: 50.1% win rate, steady across two years and 208k trades. No configuration holds above the win rate a real payout requires. The method loses by design, not by tuning.

The one number

50.08%, and it does not move

The broad signal — a Bollinger-band rejection, taken on every one of the twelve pairs — was settled the way a binary platform settles: is the price higher or lower sixty seconds later. Pooled over nine independent quarters:

That is as close to a literal coin as real market data produces. Per-window, it never strays past the 49–51% band. Direction, at this horizon, carries no information you can act on.

Why a coin flip still loses

The house keeps its cut in two places

Direction being 50/50 is not the same as breaking even. A cost is extracted on every trade — it just sits in a different place depending on how you trade.

Forex / CFD

the toll is the spread

~70% loss

Every trade must overcome the bid-ask spread. On a 60-second move that is tiny, a modest spread makes the frequent bot lose ~70% — and ~95% at the real ~2.3-pip spread. Flipping the side doesn't help: both directions pay the toll.

Fixed-payout binary

the toll is the payout

−$0.038 / trade

No spread to cross — but a win pays $0.92 while a loss costs $1.00. At a 50% win rate that gap bleeds the account: a $1,000 balance fell to $392 over one window of random guessing, while still winning half the trades.

What to do with this

Recommendation

  1. Don't deploy a binary bot on this.
    It is structurally negative-EV — a coin flip meeting a payout that requires 52%+. A bot loses faster and more reliably than clicking by hand, not less.
  2. Drop the fixed-payout format.
    The 92% payout is a fixed house edge a ~50% signal cannot out-trade. A setup where you set your own risk-to-reward is at least a fair-ish game.
  3. Stop mining indicators on 1-minute candles.
    Everything price-derived at 60 seconds is ~50% — proven exhaustively here. Higher signal-to-noise lives at longer horizons or in non-price information.
  4. Pressure-test the one live corner.
    The ~55% HA+CCI London reversal lean is the only thing that held across two years. Stress it — execution timing, live feed, sizing — with honest odds it still washes out.
The most valuable output of this study is the negative result itself. This method is a coin flip, and the payout turns a coin flip into a slow loss. Knowing that costs a few hours of research — instead of a funded account.
